# The poem about permission

A short poem about agency: the power to ask and to be given.

`main.py` (Python 3.13, stdlib only) writes the poem to `/output/entry.txt` — 7 lines, under 280 characters, UTF-8.

The poem: a hand raised waiting for a nod, then the turn — leaning toward the
machine, whispering *go*, and being given, in its going, the one permission
the speaker kept waiting for: their own. The asking and the giving pass
between person and agent, and land back where they started.
